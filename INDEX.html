Terms, Regulations & Compliance

Strict guidelines apply to all scholarship categories. Read carefully before applying.

1. Academic Excellence & Backlogs

To retain the scholarship in 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year, the student must clear all subjects in the first attempt. A single backlog or 're-appear' status will permanently terminate the grant.

2. Attendance & Conduct

Minimum 85% attendance is strictly enforced across all departments (Engineering, Medical, Science, Management). Any involvement in disciplinary cases leads to immediate suspension of funds.

3. Financial Verification

Income certificates must be issued by a Tehsildar or higher authority. Random audits are conducted, and forged documents will result in legal action and university rustication.

4. Course Specific Mandates

MBBS students must complete their mandatory clinical postings. M.Tech and M.Sc students must publish at least one research paper per academic year to qualify for renewal.
